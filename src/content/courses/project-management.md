modules:
  - code: "APM701"
    title: "Foundations for Project Management"
    duration: "12 weeks | 3 hours per week | 36 instructional hours"
    description: >
      Introduces learners to the fundamental principles, frameworks,
      methodologies, and practices of project management. Participants
      explore project lifecycles, governance, stakeholder engagement, project
      planning, scheduling, budgeting, and project execution, drawing on PMI
      standards and the PMBOK Guide.
    outcomes:
      - "Explain fundamental project management concepts, terminology, principles, and the relationship between projects, programs, and portfolios"
      - "Differentiate between predictive, agile, and hybrid project management approaches and identify appropriate applications for each"
      - "Interpret project governance structures, organizational influences, and project roles and responsibilities"
      - "Develop project charters, identify project objectives, and define project scope, deliverables, and success criteria"
      - "Identify, analyze, and develop stakeholder engagement and communication strategies"
      - "Apply project planning techniques to develop work breakdown structures (WBS), schedules, resource plans, and project budgets"
      - "Explain project monitoring and control processes, including performance measurement, change management, and reporting"
      - "Apply project management tools and techniques to develop and present a practical project management plan"
  - code: "APM702"
    title: "Project Risk and Quality Management"
    duration: "12 weeks | 3 hours per week | 36 instructional hours"
    description: >
      Equips participants with the knowledge and practical skills to
      identify, assess, prioritize, and manage project risks while
      establishing quality management practices that support successful
      project delivery. Participants explore risk frameworks, qualitative and
      quantitative analysis, risk responses, quality planning, quality
      assurance, and quality control.
    outcomes:
      - "Explain the principles, frameworks, and processes of project risk and quality management"
      - "Identify internal and external project risks, threats, opportunities and uncertainties that may affect project objectives"
      - "Develop and maintain a comprehensive project risk register, including risk ownership, categorization and prioritization"
      - "Apply qualitative and quantitative risk assessment techniques to evaluate risk probability, impact and exposure"
      - "Develop appropriate risk response strategies, contingency plans and mitigation measures"
      - "Establish project quality management plans, standards, metrics and acceptance criteria"
      - "Apply quality assurance and quality control tools, including root cause analysis, Pareto analysis, control charts and cause-and-effect diagrams"
      - "Monitor project risk and quality performance and recommend corrective and preventive actions to improve project outcomes"
  - code: "APM703"
    title: "Agile and Scrum Methodologies in Practice"
    duration: "12 weeks | 3 hours per week | 36 instructional hours"
    description: >
      Introduces participants to agile project management principles and the
      practical application of Scrum methodologies in dynamic project
      environments. Learners explore the Agile Manifesto, iterative and
      incremental delivery, Scrum accountabilities, events, artifacts,
      backlog management, sprint planning, team collaboration, and continuous
      improvement.
    outcomes:
      - "Explain the principles and values of the Agile Manifesto and distinguish agile approaches from traditional predictive project management"
      - "Evaluate different agile frameworks and methodologies, including Scrum, Kanban, and hybrid project management"
      - "Explain Scrum accountabilities, including the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Developers"
      - "Apply Scrum events, including Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective"
      - "Develop and prioritize product backlogs using user stories, acceptance criteria, and backlog refinement techniques"
      - "Apply estimation and prioritization techniques, including story points, planning poker, and relative estimation"
      - "Monitor sprint progress using burndown charts, velocity and other agile performance metrics"
      - "Facilitate agile team collaboration, stakeholder engagement, iterative delivery and continuous improvement"
