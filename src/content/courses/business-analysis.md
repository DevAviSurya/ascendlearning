The program is structured to support two professional development pathways:

- **ECBA (Entry Certificate in Business Analysis)** — develop foundational
  knowledge, terminology, techniques, and practical competencies for
  individuals entering the business analysis profession.
- **CBAP (Certified Business Analysis Professional)** — develop advanced
  analytical, strategic, leadership, and solution evaluation competencies for
  experienced business analysis professionals.
