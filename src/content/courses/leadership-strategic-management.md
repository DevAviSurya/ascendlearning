      - "Explain the principles and practices of effective interpersonal, professional, and organizational communication"

      - "Adapt communication approaches to different audiences, workplace situations and stakeholder expectations"
      - "Identify common sources, causes and dynamics of workplace conflict"
      - "Apply appropriate conflict-resolution, negotiation, mediation and problem-solving techniques"
      - "Demonstrate strategies for managing difficult conversations, giving constructive feedback and addressing workplace disagreements"
